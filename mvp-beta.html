Critical path

Any delay on these tasks delays the Beta launch one-for-one. Protect them first.

Near-critical (long lead time, start immediately): O5 store developer accounts, O1 Vietnam legal review → O4 privacy policy. Both feed store submission.

Review notes

  1. Merged E1 and E8. “100% test-covered dev flow” and “100% coverage on important flows” are the same goal. E1 sets the policy now; E8 is the gate before release.
  2. Set coverage at 100% for critical flows only (sign-up, posting, feed, reporting, payments if any). Chasing 100% everywhere slows an MVP without matching benefit.
  3. Scope freeze (S1) is the single biggest risk. Design, engineering, legal scope and budget all wait on it. Timebox it and close it with a must / should / won't list.
  4. Vietnam rules affect the build, not just paperwork. Decree 147/2024 requires social networks to verify accounts with a Vietnamese phone number. Confirm licensing, data-protection and verification duties with local counsel before E11 starts.
  5. App stores reject social apps without safety tools. User-generated content needs report, block and moderation, plus in-app account deletion (E12).
  6. Store accounts have lead time. An Apple organisation account needs a D-U-N-S number. On Google Play, new personal accounts must run a closed test with a minimum tester count for 14 days before production, so plan the Beta on closed testing.
  7. Mascot (D4) is off the critical path. It can slip past launch without blocking anything.
